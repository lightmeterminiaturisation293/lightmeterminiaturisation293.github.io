🎯 pennant - Your AI Agent, Fully Under Control

Bring any model. Pennant does the rest on your Mac and iPhone — and always asks before anything is sent, deleted or paid for.

No account required. No subscription. Runs on your own devices.

🤖 What Is Pennant?

Pennant is a free application that turns your computer into a personal AI assistant workspace. You choose the AI model — your own, a free local one, or a paid cloud one — and Pennant handles everything else: connecting to the model, running tasks, opening apps, organising files and following your instructions step by step.

Most AI tools work like a black box. You type a request and hope for the best. Pennant works differently: it is an agentic harness, which simply means it is the frame that holds an AI agent together and keeps it on a leash. Before the agent sends information somewhere, deletes a file or spends money, Pennant stops and asks you first. You stay in charge the entire time.

It is built for Apple devices — your Mac and your iPhone — and it is completely self-hosted. That means your conversations and files live on your machine, not on somebody else's server.

✨ Why People Choose Pennant

  • Bring any model. Use a free model that runs on your own computer, or connect a cloud model you already pay for. Pennant works with Ollama and with any service that speaks the OpenAI-compatible format.
  • Permission prompts, always. Nothing is sent, deleted or paid for without your explicit approval. Every risky action arrives as a simple yes-or-no question.
  • Free forever. Pennant is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. There is no paid tier, no trial countdown and no account to create.
  • Local and private. Self-hosted by design. Run everything on your own hardware and your data never leaves the room.
  • Real computer use. The agent can operate apps, work with files and complete multi-step automation jobs rather than just chatting back at you.
  • Works with MCP. Connect the tools and services you already use through the Model Context Protocol, so your agent can reach the things that matter to you.
  • Native and fast. Written in Swift with SwiftUI, so it feels like a real Apple app — smooth, responsive and built for Apple silicon.

🔧 First-Time Setup

When Pennant opens for the very first time, it walks you through a short setup. There is nothing technical to configure — you answer a few plain questions and you are done.

  • Pick your model. Choose a model that runs on your device for total privacy, or connect an outside model you already have access to. If you are unsure, start with a local one.
  • Decide what it may touch. Tell Pennant which folders and apps it is allowed to work with. You can start small and widen access later.
  • Review the permission rules. Pennant shows you exactly when it will stop and ask you for approval. Keep these on. They are your safety net.
  • Ask your first question. Type a simple request into the chat box and watch Pennant plan, act and report back — pausing whenever it needs your go-ahead.

🛡️ Your Data Stays Yours

Pennant is self-hosted. Your conversations, your files and your instructions are stored on the device you installed it on, not on a company server you cannot see. When you use a model that runs locally, nothing leaves your computer at all.

If you do connect an external model, Pennant still pauses before sending anything out. You see exactly what is about to leave your machine and you can decline. The same applies to deleting a file or spending money: the agent proposes, you decide.

💡 Things You Can Ask Pennant To Do

  • Tidy up a messy folder of documents and rename files sensibly.
  • Summarise a long set of notes and turn it into an action list.
  • Run a repetitive job across many files and report back when finished.
  • Look something up, compare the results and write a clear recommendation.
  • Connect to your own tools and services so the agent can act on real information rather than guessing.

❓ Questions People Ask

Do I need to pay for anything?

No. Pennant itself is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. If you choose to connect a paid model from another company, that company bills you separately — but Pennant never charges you and always asks before anything is paid for.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no sign-up and no email address required. Download it and open it.

What does "agentic harness" actually mean?

It is the layer that sits between you and an AI model. It gives the model tools, keeps it on track, handles the back-and-forth, and enforces your rules. Without a harness, a model can only talk. With Pennant, it can get things done — safely.

Can I use my own model?

Yes. Pennant is model-agnostic. It supports Ollama for local models and any service that offers an OpenAI-compatible interface, so you are not locked into a single provider.

Is my information safe?

Pennant runs on your own hardware and stores its data there. Every outgoing action, deletion or payment requires your approval first.

It will not open. What should I do?

Your computer may block an app downloaded from the internet the first time you open it. Confirm that you want to open it, then try again. If you still have trouble, revisit the download page and make sure you picked the file that matches your device.
